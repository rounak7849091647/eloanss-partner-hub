# Detailed, image-rich ELOANSS loan website

## Goal
Turn the existing multi-page site into a richer loan-focused experience that feels premium, modern, trustworthy, and easy to explore on desktop and mobile.

## What will change
- Upgrade the Loans overview into a visual product discovery page with a stronger image-led introduction, category filters, clearer loan summaries, a side-by-side comparison area, eligibility guidance, process steps, FAQs, and conversion sections.
- Enrich every individual loan page with relevant imagery, a quick facts panel, detailed benefits and eligibility, document checklist, fee and repayment guidance, an application timeline, EMI planning, FAQs, related products, and clear calls to apply.
- Add a focused loan comparison page so visitors can compare popular loan types and choose based on purpose, indicative rate, amount, tenure, security, and processing time.
- Improve the homepage loan content with stronger visual storytelling and direct paths into loan categories and comparison.
- Generate a cohesive set of premium finance photographs for home, personal, business, vehicle, and gold/property lending, then use them across the loan experience with meaningful alt text and efficient loading.
- Keep all existing insurance, investment, partner, contact, blog, and legal pages intact.

## Interaction and presentation
- Retain deep navy, gold, editorial typography, compact cards, and restrained motion.
- Use useful controls rather than decoration: loan-type filters, comparison selectors, EMI calculator, expandable FAQs, and clear application actions.
- Ensure mobile layouts remain readable and all buttons and navigation lead to real pages.

## Verification
- Check all new and existing loan routes, navigation, filters, comparison controls, and calculators.
- Review desktop and mobile screenshots for layout, imagery, readability, and overlap.
- Confirm page metadata, route health, browser console, and the latest build are clean.

## Assumptions
- Existing indicative rates and eligibility content remain informational and will be labeled as subject to lender assessment.
- Existing placeholder business claims and contact details will not be expanded or presented as newly verified facts.
