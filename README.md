# ELOANSS Finance Hub

Create a complete, modern, professional, and high-converting multi-page website for a financial services company called **ELOANSS**.

### Brand Overview

- Brand Name: ELOANSS

- Domain: eloanss.com

- Business Type: Loan Broker / Financial Facilitator + Insurance + Share Market services

- Tagline: “Your Trusted Partner for Loans, Insurance & Investments”

- Core Promise: Fast, transparent, and expert-guided financial solutions for individuals and businesses.

### Design Guidelines

- Style: Clean, professional, trustworthy, modern fintech look

- Primary Color: Deep Navy Blue (#0A2540)

- Secondary Color: Bright Gold / Amber (#F5A623)

- Accent Color: Soft Teal or White

- Background: Clean white with subtle light gray sections

- Typography: Modern sans-serif (Inter or Poppins)

- Layout: Mobile-first, fully responsive, spacious, high-quality icons, soft shadows, rounded cards

- Overall Feel: Premium, reliable, and easy to use
